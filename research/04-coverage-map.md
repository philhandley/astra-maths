## Checks

- Every Year 1 statutory ID (20), every Year 1 criterion (8), every Year 2 statutory ID (34) and every Year 2 criterion (10) appears above with at least one mission. 72 entries.
- Phase 1 covers the whole of place value to 100, facts to 20 with related facts, two-digit addition and subtraction, and the 2, 5 and 10 tables through arrays, which is what Year 2 autumn and early spring terms teach.
- Beyond the curriculum, kept as Playground easter eggs only: square numbers, column addition with carrying, numbers beyond 100, counting in fractions past 1 (non-statutory, in F5).
