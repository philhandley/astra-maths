# Astra Maths

A Year 1 and Year 2 maths academy for a crew of two (a child and a grown-up) on a tablet. It follows the England National Curriculum for Year 1 and Year 2 in full, in the style of Synthesis Tutor (a tutor speaks short lines on the left, a hands-on manipulative on the right) with Astra Nova touches (guess first, Big Questions with no right answer, debrief and a patch).

- 51 missions in 8 sectors, each playable at three levels (Cadet, Pilot, Commander).
- Practice is generated fresh every time and adapts: three right in a row makes it harder, a "Not yet" makes it easier and brings back the hands-on picture.
- Help on every question and hands-on step: the first tap gives a clue and changes the picture, the second ("Show me") works it through.
- "Not yet, we haven't done it at school" starts a short warm-up and, if it wobbles, suggests the earlier mission it builds on.
- Quick Fire flash cards with spaced repetition, a Fact Map of every fact, six arcade games (Crystal Crush and Bond Drop for adding and taking away are always open, the rest unlock as you go), and a Playground with every tool.
- Engineer's notes for the grown-up: a curriculum checklist, fact progress and crib notes.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step. Works offline after one visit and installs to a tablet home screen.

`PLAN.md` is the specification, `ENGINE.md` the engine contract, `research/` the curriculum sources, `DEPLOY.md` how to put it online.
